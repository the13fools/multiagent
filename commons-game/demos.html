The playroom

Tiny worlds. Strange equilibria.

The core study asks how agents protect a shared resource. These side experiments loosen one assumption at a time: must stability mean stillness? Must coordination converge? What kinds of motion can a field organize?

Live model 01 · no rest point

Boardwalk

Try two, three, then four vendors. Two settle; three chase; four form pairs. The trail makes the odd-population failure visible.

Live model 02 · phase lock

Juggling

Start at 1% timing error, 0.20 listening, and one controlled player. Raise the error, then add control back until the pattern recovers.

Watch the history, not just the snapshot. A configuration can look orderly for one instant while remaining unstable. Both applets expose the trajectory so recurring motion is not mistaken for convergence.

Conceptual demos, not grant evidence. They develop intuition and suggest future test fixtures. The reviewer path uses only Shared Resource and Common Harvest as its core examples.

The rule of the room

A question should become something you can perturb.

A demo earns its place here when changing one parameter reveals a qualitative change: settling becomes chasing, rhythm becomes collapse, or a hidden field becomes visible as motion.
