Field notes · not grant evidence

Small systems worth getting lost in.

Boardwalk and Juggling are live sketches for thinking about collective motion when “everyone settles down” is the wrong success criterion. They now live here so the Program page can stay focused on its two resource games.

Loading the side room… Two deterministic sketches are getting into motion.

01 · no rest point

Boardwalk

Two vendors settle, three keep chasing, and four form pairs. The useful question is not always how to stop motion, but how to recognize when motion is the structure.

02 · phase lock

Juggling

The pattern exists only while objects keep moving. Adjust timing error, listening, and the number of players you control to see when a small coordinated minority holds the rhythm together.

Why keep these? They are conceptual probes, not evidence for the grant. Boardwalk asks what stability means without a pure equilibrium; Juggling asks how coordination propagates through timing and shared objects.

Why move them? The scientific reviewer path now uses only Shared Resource and Common Harvest. This page can remain curious, visual, and a little less disciplined.
