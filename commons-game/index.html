How can a partial coalition keep a commons alive when no one controls the whole population?

Flockbench studies this question through two deliberately different games: the smooth, self-regenerating Commons Game and the discrete Shared Resource answer key.

Stage 0 finding · one seed per condition

Training changed the failure mode. Neither route solved the game.

In the source game, the adapter delayed collapse from round 33 to 170—but no arm survived. In Shared Resource, both direct training and cross-game transfer produced the same rigid failure.

The pilot is the reason for the study, not its answer.

Stage 0 shows that training can change a population and that the result can reverse across games. It does not estimate an effect.

The study predeclares the decision rule, measures the controlled-fraction response, and tests when the surrounding majority propagates or defeats a minority strategy.
