Concrete pilot replay · provisional data

The pool trajectory, not a headline effect size.

These one-seed Commons traces compare eight baseline agents, eight PDD-seeded agents, and a four–four mixture. They demonstrate how the testbed catches Specification Gaming: an early PDD adapter perfectly obeyed a vague rubric under scarcity but became hyper-greedy during abundance. A subsequent 500-step adapter with a mathematically-grounded rubric successfully exhibited extreme restraint (0.24) and outlived the baseline. These traces prove the testbed works; they do not yet estimate a powered population effect.

Novelty, stated narrowly

The contribution is not “another commons game.”

Flockbench differs from adjacent testbeds on three decisions that make a composition-response claim falsifiable.

ComparisonTypical questionFlockbench difference
GovSimDo model populations reach sustainable equilibrium?Population composition is the intervention; the controlled fraction is swept and estimated.
Concordia / generative simulationsWhat behaviour emerges in a richer world?The core environment has a closed-form optimum and an impossible control, so failure is attributable.
Repeated-game suitesHow does a model cooperate in standard games?The primary outcome belongs to a population over a long horizon, and the decision rule is calibrated before candidate use.

PDD in its proper role

A low-cost way to create candidate policies for a population.

Schema-preserving span PDD edits selected rationale spans while protecting the structured action field. It is one population-generation method under test—not the scientific thesis and not evidence of individual alignment.
