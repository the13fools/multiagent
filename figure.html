What each panel is doing

A — why a solution exists at all. The most an agent can afford to restore is flat in G; what the pool needs falls with it. Where they cross, a strategy exists under which everyone survives. The shaded wedge is slack — the free-riders a flock can carry — and it is closed-form, which is what gives the empirical threshold an answer key.

B — the same population, two update rules. Identical arithmetic, identical seats. One herds into lockstep and starves; the other holds. The number underneath is the restore-rate gap: distance from the answer that was available the whole time.

C — the result a reviewer will not believe from a table. Probability a gate rolls back a candidate identical to its baseline. The overlap-rule line sits at 1.0 and does not come down as the campaign grows, because it bounds a proportion that converges to one half. Only non-inferiority crosses the 5% line.

Every value is computed live from src/core, the same modules the test suite pins to flockbench. Increase gate trials before the final export — 2,000 is smooth enough to read and 10,000 is smooth enough to print.
